# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Active Players**: Amateur and grassroots football players at PINT FC checking kickoff times, venue directions, countdowns, and submitting/updating their matchday attendance (Going / Maybe / Not Going) before the cutoff deadline.
- **Captains & Match Organizers**: Coordinators running matchday attendance, projecting live headcounts in real-time on club screens, and reviewing attendee availability with private notes visible only to leadership.
- **Supporters & Community**: Fans following PINT FC's 2026/27 campaign, checking latest league/friendly scores, league standings table, and squad directory.

## Product Purpose

PINT FC eliminates chaotic group chat messaging and unorganized spreadsheets by providing a single, focused, high-contrast matchday attendance and club hub. Players confirm their availability in one tap; captains see exact squad numbers in real-time.

## Positioning

Unlike bloated league management software or ad-heavy sports apps, PINT FC is tailored specifically for grassroots weekend football: instant load times, zero public PII leaks, strict minor safeguards with guardian consent verification, private teammate attendance notes, and automated RSVP cutoffs.
